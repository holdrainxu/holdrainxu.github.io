I am a PhD candidate (combined Master–PhD program, currently in my second year) in **Geotechnical Engineering** at the **School of Infrastructure Engineering**, [Dalian University of Technology](https://en.dlut.edu.cn/), affiliated with the **Institute of Rock Instability and Seismicity Research**. The institute is led by Prof.&nbsp;Chun-an Tang (Cheung Kong Distinguished Professor of the Ministry of Education; Recipient of the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars of China). My doctoral research is supervised by Prof.&nbsp;Shibin Tang (National Young Top-Notch Talent).

My work sits at the interface of **rock mechanics** and **artificial intelligence**, with a focus on turning noisy, multimodal field data into reliable engineering insight. I am particularly interested in:

- **Digital twin** for underground/slope engineering — building geometry-, photometry- and physics-aware tunnel & rock-mass models from LiDAR, photogrammetry, and microseismic streams.
- **Diffusion models for engineering signals** — using generative priors to denoise weak microseismic precursors, and to drive spatiotemporal early-warning of **rockburst** in deep hard-rock projects.
- **Vision-based slope deformation monitoring** — flexible, large-area, low-cost, automated monitoring of high steep slopes and tunnel surrounding rock from commodity cameras.
- **Geological-hazard perception** — landslide, rockfall and debris-flow detection on vehicle-borne / mobile platforms.

To date I have authored **three first-author SCI papers** in CAS Tier-1 / Tier-2 Top journals (Adv. Eng. Inform., IJRMMS, Bull. Eng. Geol. Environ. — cumulative IF 21.6), hold **5 granted invention patents** as the student first inventor, and have **10+ additional published patents**. I am a core technical contributor on 4 NSFC / national projects (≈ 3.37 M CNY total) and the **sole technical lead** on 2 industry-sponsored projects. I also serve as the doctoral student Party-branch secretary of our research institute.

I am actively looking for **academic exchange** and **postdoctoral** opportunities at strong groups working on AI for geo-engineering, computer vision for monitoring, or generative models for scientific signals. If our interests overlap, please get in touch.
